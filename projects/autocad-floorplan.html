AutoCAD Floor Plan Drafting

Self-Directed Practice · AutoCAD Fundamentals

Tools: AutoCAD · 2D drafting · layers · hatching · dimensioning

Overview

To prepare for my internship at Gallo, I challenged myself to draft a complete set of 2D & 3D floor plans resembeling my grandparents house back home. During my first internship at S+B James Construction, there was always a general rule of 3 views limit in drawings.

Result

Although this project wasn't as extensive as my other ones, it helped me build the foundational skills for drafting real spaces from real-life to CAD. As I write this, I'm currently close on two months as a intern at Gallo and this project especially helped me to illustrate my documentation as clean as possible that way when it is carried over to other staff members, they can interpret my goal of the drawing.
